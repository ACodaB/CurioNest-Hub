About

Welcome to CurioNest Hub, a platform dedicated to making science education engaging, accessible, and effective through innovative teaching and learning techniques.

Founded by Manisha Bajaj, an experienced Science and Chemistry educator with over 19 years of teaching expertise, CurioNest is built on the understanding that every child learns differently and possesses a unique attention span.

Through mentorship-focused learning, students are introduced to:

  • Interactive experiments
  • Root-word language analysis to ignite scientific thinking
  • A genuine thirst for knowledge
  • Real-world learning experiences
  • Deep academic confidence

As an educator, my vision is to present an environment of happy learning that is conducive to students' all-round development. I act as a facilitator to help them open their minds to new thoughts and information, and partner closely with parents to ensure transparent progress tracking and a supportive learning journey for every student.
